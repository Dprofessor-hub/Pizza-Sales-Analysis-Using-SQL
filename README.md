## Overview

The Pizza Sales Analysis project is a SQL-based business analytics project designed to analyze sales performance, customer ordering patterns, product popularity, and revenue trends for a pizza restaurant.

Using relational database concepts and SQL queries, the project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights. The analysis helps identify top-performing pizzas, customer preferences, revenue drivers, and operational trends that can support better business decisions.

---

## Business Problem

Restaurant businesses generate large volumes of transactional data every day. Understanding customer purchasing behavior and sales performance is essential for improving profitability and optimizing operations.

This project aims to answer key business questions such as:

* How many orders were placed?
* What is the total revenue generated?
* Which pizzas are the most popular?
* Which pizza sizes are ordered most frequently?
* What are the best-performing pizza categories?
* How does revenue vary across products and categories?
* What are the peak ordering periods?

---

## Dataset Information

The project uses four related tables:

### Orders

Stores order date and time information.

### Order Details

Stores pizza quantities for each order.

### Pizzas

Contains pizza size and pricing information.

### Pizza Types

Contains pizza names, categories, and ingredients.
